Research directions
◇

Metabolic crosstalk in MASH-associated HCC

Liver zonation, arginine metabolism, lipid uptake, and metabolic communication between steatotic liver tissue and tumor cells.

Arginine · Lipid metabolism · HCC
◎

Oncofetal regulatory T cells and immune resistance

Developmental programs, metabolic adaptation, and spatial immune niches that influence Treg-mediated suppression in liver cancer.

Treg · OXPHOS · Spatial niches

Cancer organoid genetics and cell-state plasticity

Organoids · CRISPR · Single-cell
